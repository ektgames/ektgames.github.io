Privacy Policy

Game: Pulsevania (iOS / iPadOS) • Publisher: EKT Games • Last Updated: 22 July 2026

1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy describes how EKT Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles data in connection with your download, installation, and use of our mobile game Pulsevania on Apple iOS and iPadOS devices.

We respect your privacy and are dedicated to ensuring that your personal data is handled responsibly and in full compliance with applicable privacy regulations, including the UK Data Protection Act 2018, the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), EU GDPR, and Apple App Store Review Guidelines.

2. Information We Collect & How We Collect It

Pulsevania has been designed to minimise data collection. We do not require you to register an account, enter your real name, or provide your physical address or telephone number to play the game.

  • Local Save Data: Your gameplay progress, unlocked equipment, game settings, and audio preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Apple Game Center Integration (Optional): If you choose to enable Apple Game Center on your iOS device, Game Center may process your Apple ID alias, leaderboards, and achievements.
  • Anonymised Technical Diagnostics: If permitted by your device settings, Apple provides us with aggregated, non-identifiable crash reports via App Store Connect.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for monetary consideration or targeted marketing purposes.

The game interacts only with Apple infrastructure for core operations:

  • Apple App Store & TestFlight: For application deployment, security verification, and update distribution.
  • Apple Game Center: For leaderboards and achievement syncing.

4. Children's Privacy

Pulsevania is suitable for general audiences. We comply strictly with the UK Age-Appropriate Design Code and COPPA. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
