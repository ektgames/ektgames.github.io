Your Privacy Choices

Understand how to control your data, manage permissions, and exercise your privacy rights for Pulsevania on iOS.

iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS 14.5 or later, Apple requires apps to ask for your permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps or websites. You can change your tracking preferences at any time directly through your iOS device settings.

To change tracking permissions on iPhone or iPad:
  1. Open the Settings app on your Apple device.
  2. Tap Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  3. Toggle permission for Pulsevania ON or OFF according to your preference.

Apple Game Center Privacy

If you use Apple Game Center for score leaderboards or achievements in Pulsevania, your profile visibility is controlled by your Apple ID account settings.

To manage Game Center profile privacy:
  1. Open Settings on your iOS device.
  2. Scroll down and tap Game Center.
  3. Adjust Profile Privacy (Everyone, Friends Only, or Only You).

Local Game Save & Data Removal

All save files, player statistics, and custom configurations are stored locally on your device storage.

To completely remove all local data:

Simply press and hold the Pulsevania app icon on your iOS home screen, tap Remove App, and confirm Delete App. This permanently purges all local save data.

Submit a Data Request to EKT Games

If you have questions about your privacy choices or wish to formally submit a privacy request, our team is available to assist you.
