Frequently Asked Questions

How is my save data stored?
Pulsevania automatically saves your progress locally on your iOS device. Progress is saved at checkpoints, room transitions, and upon defeating major bosses.
Does Pulsevania support game controllers?
Yes! Pulsevania supports MFi (Made for iPhone) controllers, Xbox Wireless Controllers, and PlayStation DualSense/DualShock 4 controllers paired via Bluetooth to your iOS device.
The game is lagging or crashing. How can I improve performance?
Ensure your device is running iOS 14.0 or later. Close background applications to free up RAM, check that Low Power Mode is disabled, and restart your iOS device.
How do I request a refund for an App Store purchase?
All financial transactions and billing are handled directly by Apple. To request a refund, please visit Apple's official portal at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I report a bug or issue?
If you encounter a bug, glitch, or unexpected behavior in Pulsevania, please email our developer team at support@ektgames.com with your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue.

Still need assistance?

Our support team is here to help you get back into the action. Send us a message and we'll reply as soon as possible.
